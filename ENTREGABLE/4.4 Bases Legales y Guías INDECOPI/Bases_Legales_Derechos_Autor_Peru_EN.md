# LEGAL FRAMEWORK FOR SOFTWARE PROTECTION (PERU & ANDEAN COMMUNITY)

This document describes the current legal framework in the Republic of Peru and the member countries of the **Andean Community (CAN)** that supports the protection of intellectual and industrial property rights of **SportMatch Connect**.

---

## 📜 1. Legislative Decree No. 822 — Copyright Law (Peru)

Software (logical support) is assimilated under the category of **literary works** and is regulated under the following guidelines:

*   **Article 2 (Definition):** *Logical Support (Computer Program)* is defined as the expression of a set of instructions through words, codes, plans, or in any other form, which, when incorporated into an automated reading device, is capable of causing a computer to perform a task or achieve a result. It also includes technical documentation and user manuals.
*   **Article 5 (Inception of Right):** Copyright protection is born with the creation of the work, independent of its merit, destination, or registration formalities. Registration before INDECOPI is **declarative** in nature (providing proof of date and authorship against third parties) and not constitutive.
*   **Articles 16 and 23 (Ownership in Works Made for Hire / Employment Relationship):** Unless agreed otherwise, it is presumed that the financial rights (commercial exploitation) of software developed in the academic or organizational scope under employment are transferred to the employer or applicant (in this case, Universidad San Ignacio de Loyola - USIL), while the students retain their inalienable **moral rights** (recognition of authorship and integrity of the work).

---

## 📜 2. Decision 351 of the Andean Community (Common Regime on Copyright)

Establishes a uniform transnational protection within the member countries (Bolivia, Colombia, Ecuador, and Peru):

*   **Article 3:** Logical support is protected under the same terms as literary works.
*   **Source Code and Object Code Protection:** Protection extends to both human-readable instructions (source code) and machine-executable instructions (object code), regardless of the form or medium of its fixation.

---

## 📜 3. Decision 486 of the Andean Community (Common Regime on Industrial Property)

Regulates access to invention patents in the Andean territory:

*   **Article 15, literal e) (Exclusions from Patentability):** Computer programs or logical support, **"as such"**, are not considered inventions.
*   **Boundary Line for CII (Computer-Implemented Inventions):** If a software proposal does not operate in isolation, but controls a physical process, transforms external electromagnetic signals, or interacts with physical hardware achieving a **measurable and novel technical effect**, such an invention is patentable as a **Computer-Implemented Invention (CII)**. In SportMatch Connect, the predictive geospatial matchmaking algorithms and real-time audio/moderation processing on the client comply with these technical effect guidelines.
