## 📋 CHAPTER I: PROBLEM STATEMENT

### 1.1. Description of the Problem Reality
In Lima Metropolitana, 72% of the adult population performs insufficient physical activity (MINSA 2024), which worsens public health issues such as sedentary lifestyles and cardiovascular diseases. Despite the existence of sports fields and a high demand for amateur recreation (football, padel, basketball), there is no unified channel for organizing matches. Coordination is done through informal messaging groups (WhatsApp/Telegram) where information is dispersed, players are not filtered by skill level, organizers assume individual financial debts to book courts, and mobile wallet collection generates debt collection friction.

### 1.2. Problem Formulation
*   **General Problem:** How does the design and implementation of an information platform based on predictive matchmaking and artificial intelligence influence coordination efficiency and the continuity of amateur sports practice among young adults in Lima Metropolitana during 2026?

### 1.3. Research Objectives
*   **General Objective:** Develop and implement "SportMatch Connect", a geolocated sports matchmaking platform with a gamified economy and intelligent assistant to unify and optimize amateur sports practice in Lima.
*   **Specific Objectives:**
    1.  Design a multivariable predictive algorithm for player skill leveling.
    2.  Implement geo-spatial searches and bookings integrated with a PostGIS database.
    3.  Build a split payment system based on FitCoins and the Stripe gateway.
    4.  Design a multimodal conversational voice assistant (Gemini AI).

## 📚 CHAPTER II: THEORETICAL FRAMEWORK AND STATE-OF-THE-ART

### 2.1. Research Antecedents
*   *Playtomic (Spain):* A reference in padel booking automation, with a transactional focus but limited in adapted predictive algorithms and financial gamification in the Andean region.
*   *CourtSide (USA):* Focused on static matchmaking, without real-time conversational support or edge moderation.

### 2.2. Theoretical Foundations
*   **Haversine Algorithm:** To calculate spherical distances on the Earth's surface given latitude and longitude.
*   **Elo Rating System:** A mathematical model to estimate the relative skill of players dynamically after each match.
*   **Feature-Sliced Design (FSD):** A frontend architecture methodology that divides the application into structured layers (app, routes, widgets, features, entities, shared).
